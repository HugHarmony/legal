Text Messaging Terms & Conditions

These terms describe the text message (SMS) program operated by Hug Harmony LLC ("Hug Harmony," "we," "us," or "our").

Effective date: October 21, 2026

Program description

When you opt in, Hug Harmony sends transactional text messages about your activity on our platform, including: booking confirmations, booking changes and cancellations, session reminders, and alerts that you have a new in-platform message. We do not send marketing or promotional text messages under this program.

How to opt in

You opt in by creating a Hug Harmony account, providing your mobile phone number, and agreeing to receive text notifications by checking the consent box at sign-up or in your account settings. Consent is not a condition of purchasing any service.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies based on your bookings and messages. You may receive several messages around an active booking and none when you have no activity.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier and plan. Hug Harmony does not charge a separate fee for text messages.

How to opt out

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving text messages from Hug Harmony. You may receive one confirmation message after opting out.

How to get help

Reply HELP to any message, or contact us at founder@hugharmony.com.

Supported carriers

Messages are available on most major U.S. carriers. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

Your use of the text program is covered by our Privacy Policy. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes, and text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.
