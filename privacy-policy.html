Privacy Policy

Hug Harmony LLC ("Hug Harmony," "we," "us," or "our") operates an online platform that connects clients with independent professional cuddlers for platonic cuddle sessions. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Effective date: October 21, 2026

Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, and mobile phone number when you create an account or book a session.
  • Booking information: session details such as date, time, the provider or client you booked with, and messages exchanged through the platform.
  • Payment information: payments are processed by our third-party payment processor. We do not store full card numbers on our systems.
  • Technical information: device, browser, and usage data collected automatically when you use our website or app.

How we use your information

  • To operate the platform, create and manage your account, and complete bookings.
  • To send service-related notifications, including booking confirmations, booking changes and cancellations, session reminders, and alerts that you have a new in-platform message.
  • To keep the platform safe, including verification, fraud prevention, and enforcing our terms.
  • To respond to your questions and support requests.

Text messaging (SMS)

When you provide your mobile phone number and opt in, we send transactional text messages about your bookings and messages — for example, booking confirmations, changes, cancellations, reminders, and new-message alerts. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP, and get help by replying HELP.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. We share mobile information only with the service providers that deliver the messages on our behalf, and only so they can perform that service.

How we share information

  • With the other party to your booking: limited booking details are shared between the client and provider taking part in a session so the session can happen.
  • With service providers: companies that help us run the platform (such as hosting, payment processing, and message delivery) under agreements that limit their use of your information.
  • For legal reasons: if required by law, court order, or to protect the safety of our users.

We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices

  • You may update or delete your account information in your account settings or by contacting us.
  • You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. You may still receive messages needed to complete a booking already in progress.
  • You may unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link in any marketing email.

Data security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's privacy

Hug Harmony is for adults 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The current version will always be posted at this URL with its effective date.
